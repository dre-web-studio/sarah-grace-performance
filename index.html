FAQ

Questions people ask before booking.

A few common questions to help you choose the right performance style for your event.

Do you offer stilt walking for corporate events in Nashville?

Yes. Sarah Grace Performance offers stilt walking for corporate events, galas, brand activations, conferences, grand openings, holiday parties, and private celebrations in Nashville and surrounding areas.

Can I book fire performance for a private event?

Yes. Fire performance may be available for outdoor private events, festivals, nightlife events, and special celebrations depending on venue rules, safety space, weather, and event logistics.

Do you offer indoor entertainment options?

Yes. LED performance and stilt walking can be strong options for indoor venues, corporate events, parties, and brand activations where fire may not be permitted.

Can performances be customized to a theme or brand?

Yes. Costuming, colors, performance style, music, and guest interaction can be adapted to support the event theme, brand identity, season, or atmosphere.

What types of events are a good fit?

Sarah Grace Performance is a strong fit for corporate events, brand activations, galas, festivals, fairs, weddings, holiday parties, VIP receptions, grand openings, nightlife events, and immersive guest experiences.
