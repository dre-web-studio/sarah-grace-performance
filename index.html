The artist

Meet Sarah Grace.

Sarah Grace is a Nashville-based movement artist specializing in stilt walking, fire performance, and immersive event presence.

Her work is built for rooms where atmosphere matters: luxury celebrations, corporate productions, music industry events, private parties, and brand moments that need more than ordinary entertainment.

Every appearance can be tailored around costuming, guest interaction, event timing, venue layout, and the mood you want guests to feel the second they arrive.
